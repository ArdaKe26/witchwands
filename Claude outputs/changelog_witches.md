## 1.0.0 – Wand Witches 🧙

All four wands are now obtainable in Survival! This update adds the rare **Wand Witches** who carry them.

### Added
- 🧙 **Wand Witches**: a rare new kind of witch that carries one of the four wands and fights with its power.
  - 🔥 **Fire Witch**: spawns in **Nether fortresses** and shoots fireballs.
  - ❄️ **Ice Witch**: spawns on **frozen and jagged peaks** and throws bursts of slowing snowballs.
  - 🌀 **Teleport Witch**: spawns in **End cities**. She pulls you to her from up to 30 blocks away (locking your ender pearls for 30s), blinks away when hit up close, and throws potions.
  - 🌱 **Soil Witch**: spawns in **abandoned villages** (villages with no villagers left). She heals hurt monsters nearby every 5s and throws potions.
- Wand Witches have a **10% chance** to drop their wand when defeated.
- Wand Witches still drink potions like normal witches, then take their wand back out.
- **Wand Witch Spawn Egg**, added to the Witch Wands creative tab.

### Changed
- Wands can now be obtained in Survival by defeating Wand Witches.
- Updated the mod description and README with installation and usage instructions.
