# 🧙 Witch Wands

**Four magical wands. Four rare witches who guard them.**

Witch Wands adds four elemental wands to Minecraft, each with two unique abilities. But you can't craft them: you'll have to track down the rare **Wand Witches** who carry them, survive their magic, and hope they drop their wand.

---

## 🪄 The Wands

### 🔥 Fire Wand
- **Fireball** (right-click): launches a fireball that explodes on impact, sets mobs on fire and deals splash damage, **without breaking blocks**. 5s cooldown.
- **Inferno** (Shift + right-click): sets every mob within 25 blocks on fire. 20s cooldown.

### ❄️ Ice Wand
- **Snowball Spray** (right-click, hold): sprays snowballs that deal damage and slow enemies down. 30 shots, then a 15s reload, with a live ammo counter.
- **Freeze** (Shift + right-click): freezes everyone within 25 blocks in place for 5 seconds. 30s cooldown.

### 🌀 Teleport Wand
- **Teleport** (right-click): instantly teleport to the block you're looking at, up to 30 blocks away. No fall damage. 7s cooldown.
- **Pull** (Shift + right-click): yank the mob or player you're looking at right in front of you, and lock their ender pearls for 30 seconds so they can't escape. Blocked by walls. 35s cooldown.

### 🌱 Soil Wand
*A peaceful wand for farmers.*
- **Food Give** (right-click): look at a wheat, carrot, potato or beetroot crop to get 10 of that food. 10s cooldown.
- **Grow** (Shift + right-click while standing on farmland): instantly grows your entire connected farm (up to 20 blocks) and plants empty farmland with the seeds in your offhand. **1 seed plants 2 blocks!** 15s cooldown.

Every wand also works as a melee weapon, has its own sounds and particles, and shows cooldowns above your hotbar.

---

## 🧙 Wand Witches

The only way to get a wand in Survival is to defeat the **Wand Witch** who carries it. Each one spawns rarely, only in her own place, and fights with her wand's power:

| Witch | Where to find her | How she fights |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Fire Witch** | Nether fortresses | Shoots fireballs at you |
| ❄️ **Ice Witch** | Frozen & jagged mountain peaks | Throws bursts of slowing snowballs |
| 🌀 **Teleport Witch** | End cities | Pulls you to her from 30 blocks away, locks your pearls, and blinks away when you get close |
| 🌱 **Soil Witch** | Abandoned villages (no villagers left) | Heals every hurt monster around her, so take her out first! |

- Wand Witches are **rare**, so be patient when hunting them.
- They have a **10% chance** to drop their wand when defeated.
- They still drink potions like normal witches, so watch out!

## 📦 Installation

1. Install **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/)** for **Minecraft 1.21.1**.
2. Download **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)** (required).
3. Download **Witch Wands** from the Versions tab.
4. Put both `.jar` files in your `mods` folder (Windows: press `Win + R` and type `%appdata%\.minecraft\mods`).
5. Launch Minecraft with the **fabric-loader-1.21.1** profile.

## 🎮 How to use

- **Creative:** open the **Witch Wands** creative tab. It has all four wands and a Wand Witch spawn egg.
- **Survival:** hunt the Wand Witches in their spawn locations (see the table above).
- **Controls:** right-click for the main ability, Shift + right-click for the second ability, and hit to use it as a melee weapon.
- **Soil Wand tip:** for Grow, stand on farmland with seeds in your offhand (press `F` to swap).
